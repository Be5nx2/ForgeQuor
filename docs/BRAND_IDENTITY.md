# Forgequor — Brand Identity & Visual Guidelines

## 1. Project Identity

- **Official Name:** Forgequor
- **Stylized Name:** FORGEQUOR
- **Name Origin:** Forge + Quorum
- **Category:** Multi-Agent AI Game Development Orchestrator
- **Tagline:** Many Minds. One Forge. Infinite Worlds.
- **Brand Language:** English

### Project Overview

Forgequor is a multi-agent AI orchestrator dedicated to video game design and development.

It brings together multiple specialized AI agents working collaboratively within a unified orchestration environment.

The project's vision is to create an intelligent game development ecosystem where specialized agents cooperate to design, implement, test, optimize, and maintain video games.

### Technical Ecosystem

- Godot Engine 4.7
- Claude Code
- OpenAI Codex
- Future integration of open-source LLMs
- Multi-agent architecture
- Model-agnostic architecture
- Modular and extensible infrastructure
- Collaborative intelligence
- Automated game development workflows

Forgequor's brand identity must remain independent of any specific game engine, AI model, or technology provider.

## 10. Brand Consistency Guidelines

### Do

- Always use the correct spelling: Forgequor.
- Use FORGEQUOR for graphical wordmarks.
- Maintain the electric blue and forge orange color contrast.
- Preserve the relationship between forging, artificial intelligence, and video game development.
- Favor dark and cinematic backgrounds.
- Maintain excellent readability at different sizes.
- Use consistent typography.
- Maintain a professional and futuristic appearance.
- Keep the brand independent of technology providers.
- Preserve the original artwork as a visual reference.

### Do Not

- Modify the spelling of Forgequor.
- Integrate third-party logos into the permanent brand identity.
- Include AI model names in the primary logo.
- Include game engine version numbers in the permanent logo.
- Use excessive visual effects.
- Introduce unrelated colors.
- Stretch or distort the logo.
- Overload the design with unnecessary details.
- Sacrifice readability for visual complexity.

When regenerating or extending Forgequor's visual identity using generative AI:

1. Provide the original banner as a visual reference.
2. Provide this brand identity document as context.
3. Use the master image generation prompt.
4. Specify the required asset dimensions and aspect ratio.
5. Preserve the original color palette.
6. Maintain the primary emblem's symbolic elements.
7. Preserve the wordmark typography and color treatment.
8. Verify the exact spelling of FORGEQUOR.
9. Ensure visual consistency with existing branding assets.

Using both the original artwork and the written brand guidelines improves visual consistency, although exact reproduction is not guaranteed.
